How It Works

Bankroll Tracker Rules

Official Entries Only

Only entries officially submitted by PlayMaker are added to the tracker. Visitors cannot submit or modify picks.

Every Result Counts

Winning and losing entries remain permanently visible. Results are not removed based on performance.

Bankroll Calculation

Winning entries add the net profit to the bankroll. Losing entries subtract the full wager amount. Voided entries produce no profit or loss.

Pending Entries

Pending wagers do not affect settled profit and loss until the entry receives an official final result.
