Matchup Intelligence Logic

The matchup board should help users decide where to start, not force every game into a pick.

01

Defensive Weakness

Does one team allow production in a specific prop category?

02

Player Trend Strength

Are the relevant players showing strong recent usage or hit-rate patterns?

03

Weather Safety

Are conditions clean enough for passing, rushing, receiving, or kicking markets?

04

Injury Clarity

Are key players healthy, limited, or creating role shifts for backups?

05

Game Script

Does the likely pace, score environment, or spread support the prop angle?
