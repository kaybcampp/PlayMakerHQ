Players Hub FAQ

Common questions about PlayMaker player trends, reports, and matchup intelligence.

How are PlayMaker player trends calculated?

PlayMaker player trends are built from player role, recent performance, position, opponent context, prop category, and matchup-specific indicators.

How often are player reports updated?

Player intelligence reports are designed to update weekly during the NFL season as schedules, roles, matchups, injuries, and results change.

How does PlayMaker identify opportunities?

PlayMaker looks for opportunity signals like increased usage, favorable defensive matchups, red-zone role, injury-related volume, recent trend changes, and prop category fit.

How are defensive matchups measured?

Defensive matchups use team defensive tiers, pass and run defense grades, pressure rate, historical prop results, and position-specific matchup tendencies.

What data sources power PlayMaker?

PlayMaker uses roster data, schedule data, defensive tiers, historical PlayMaker results, player research files, matchup context, and tool-generated prop analysis.

Can I research NFL players before using PlayMaker?

Yes. Every Player Intelligence Report is designed to help you research NFL players before opening PlayMaker. Review recent trends, matchup analysis, defensive context, prop-focused insights, historical performance, and AI-generated intelligence to better understand each player's outlook before entering a prop line into the PlayMaker tool.
