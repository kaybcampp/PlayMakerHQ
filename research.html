Research Signals

Quick filters PlayMaker considers before making a recommendation.

Green Flags

  • Stable usage trend
  • Favorable matchup tier
  • Consistent snap share
  • Clean injury report

Yellow Flags

  • Role change or snap drop
  • Neutral matchup
  • Weather uncertainty
  • Recent volatility

Red Flags

  • Low usage trend
  • Elite opposing defense
  • Injury limitation
  • Unclear game script
