Research Signals

Quick indicators that help PlayMaker separate stronger plays from forced picks.

Green Signals

  • Strong recent hit rate
  • Favorable defense tier
  • Stable player usage
  • Clean injury context

Warning Signals

  • Role change or snap drop
  • Bad weather conditions
  • Difficult matchup tier
  • Line inflated by public hype

Fade Signals

  • Low recent volume
  • Elite opposing defense
  • Injury-limited player
  • Unclear game script
