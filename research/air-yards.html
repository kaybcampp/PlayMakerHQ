What are Air Yards? Explosive Play Indicator

Air Yards measure how far the ball travels in the air on passing targets. It is one of the strongest indicators of deep threat usage and explosive fantasy upside.

Air Yards Definition

Understanding downfield opportunity in the passing game.

Definition

Air Yards = total yards the ball travels in the air on pass attempts to a player.

It does NOT include yards after the catch — only the distance the ball travels before the receiver touches it.

Why Air Yards Matter for Props

Deep targets create higher upside even with low catch volume.

01

Explosive Play Potential

02

03

04
