Last 10 Games vs Season Average Form vs Baseline Explained

This metric compares a player’s recent performance (last 10 games) against their full-season average to identify rising or declining trends.

What This Metric Means

Separating short-term form from long-term performance.

Definition

Last 10 vs Season Average = comparison between recent performance and full-season baseline.

It helps identify whether a player is trending up, trending down, or staying consistent.

Why It Matters for Props

Recency can signal real role changes — or fake streaks.

01

Detects Role Changes

Usage increases often show up before season averages adjust.

02

Filters Out Noise

One big game doesn’t define a full season trend.

03

Identifies Breakouts Early

Players trending up often outperform projections before markets adjust.

04

Avoids Regression Traps

Hot streaks without usage support often regress quickly.

Real NFL Example

Why recent form alone can be misleading.

Example

Last 10 Games: 80 YPG vs Season Avg: 55 YPG

This indicates a true usage or efficiency increase. If supported by snap share and target share, it signals a real breakout — not randomness.
