How to Use NFL Leaderboards

No single metric tells the entire story.

01

Start With Opportunity

Metrics such as target share and air yards help establish how much opportunity a player is receiving before evaluating efficiency.

02

Evaluate Efficiency

EPA and Success Rate provide additional context about how efficiently players and offenses turn opportunities into productive plays.

03

Check the Environment

Defensive matchups, pressure, and pace can change the environment surrounding an otherwise strong offensive opportunity.

04

Put It Together

The strongest analysis comes from combining multiple signals rather than relying on one leaderboard in isolation.
