Why Position Matters in Betting

Different positions require different modeling logic.

Usage is Position-Specific

A WR’s value = targets. An RB’s value = carries + red zone work.

Efficiency Differs by Role

QB EPA, WR YPRR, RB yards per carry all measure different truths.

Matchups Affect Positions Differently

Defenses impact QBs, WRs, and RBs in completely different ways.

Game Script Shapes Everything

Trailing teams favor QB/WR volume, leading teams favor RB efficiency.
