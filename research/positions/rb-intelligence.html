RB Intelligence evaluates running backs based on workload, efficiency, red zone usage, receiving involvement, and matchup strength — not just rushing yards.

Core RB Metrics

The foundation of running back evaluation in PlayMaker.

01

Carry Share

Percentage of team rushing attempts assigned to the RB.

02

Rushing Efficiency

Yards per carry adjusted for defensive strength and game script.

03

Snap Share

Measures how often the RB is on the field overall.

04

Target Involvement

Receiving usage adds stability and upside in all game scripts.

Scoring & Red Zone Usage

Touchdowns drive RB fantasy and prop ceilings.

01

Red Zone Carries

Inside-the-20 opportunities are the highest value rushes.

02

Goal Line Role

Determines touchdown dependency and scoring consistency.

03

Touchdown Conversion Rate

How often opportunities turn into actual scores.

04

Script Dependency

RBs in leading teams get more red zone opportunities.

Matchup Factors

How defenses impact running back production.

01

Run Defense Strength

Elite run defenses reduce rushing efficiency and volume.

02

Game Script Dependency

RB usage drops in negative pass-heavy scripts.

03

O-Line Advantage

Blocking quality significantly impacts RB success rate.

04

Pace of Play Influence

Faster offenses increase total RB touch opportunities.
