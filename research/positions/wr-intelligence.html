WR Intelligence Wide Receiver Performance Model

WR Intelligence evaluates wide receivers based on target share, air yards, efficiency, YAC, route usage, and matchup strength — not just receptions or yards.

Core WR Metrics

The foundation of wide receiver evaluation in PlayMaker.

01

Target Share

Percentage of team passing targets directed to the WR.

02

Air Yards

Downfield opportunity volume and explosive play potential.

03

YPRR (Efficiency)

Yards per route run — one of the strongest WR efficiency signals.

04

Route Participation

How often the WR is actually running routes vs blocking or rotating out.

Explosive Play Ability

WRs win props through big plays and YAC production.

01

YAC (After Catch)

Ability to turn short targets into long gains.

02

Separation Ability

How consistently a WR gets open vs coverage.

03

Deep Target Rate

Frequency of high-value downfield opportunities.

04

Big Play Dependency

How reliant production is on explosive receptions.

Matchup Factors

How defenses impact wide receiver production.

01

Coverage Quality

Elite CBs reduce target efficiency and separation.

02

Slot vs Outside Matchups

Different WR alignments face different defensive weaknesses.

03

Game Script Volume Boost

Negative scripts increase WR target share significantly.

04

Pressure Impact on Timing

QB pressure can reduce deep shot opportunities.
