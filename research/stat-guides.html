Why These Stats Matter

Box-score production tells you what already happened. Usage, opportunity, and efficiency help explain the context behind it.

Core Idea

Volume creates opportunity. Efficiency creates upside.

These metrics help separate repeatable player involvement from short-term production and unstable results.

How PlayMaker Uses These Stats

Each metric contributes a different layer of player and matchup evaluation.

01

Usage Baseline

Snap share, target share, and carry share establish how much opportunity a player is earning.

02

Efficiency Layer

Air yards, yards per route run, EPA, YAC, and Success Rate add quality and efficiency context.

03

Context Layer

Defensive pressure, matchup quality, weather, injuries, and expected game script adjust the baseline.

04

Final Prop Evaluation

PlayMaker combines player trends and matchup context to support Ride, Fade, or Avoid decisions.
