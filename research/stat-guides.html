Why These Stats Matter

Most bettors look at yards and touchdowns. PlayMaker looks at usage, opportunity, and efficiency.

Core Idea

Volume creates opportunity. Efficiency creates upside.

These stats explain both — and help you predict future performance instead of reacting to past results.
