Success Rate Definition

Understanding consistency over volatility.

Definition

Success Rate measures the percentage of qualifying plays that meet the expected efficiency threshold for the situation.

Unlike raw yardage, Success Rate focuses on how consistently an offense creates useful outcomes across individual plays.

Why Success Rate Matters for Props

Consistency is often more important than isolated explosive games.

01

Stability Indicator

High Success Rate can indicate a more consistent offensive baseline instead of production dependent on isolated big plays.

02

Volatility Context

Success Rate helps distinguish steady production from performance driven primarily by explosive plays.

03

Drive Sustainability

Efficient plays can help offenses maintain possession and sustain drives.

04

Prop Signal

Success Rate provides another consistency layer when evaluating player and team prop environments.

Real NFL Example

Why consistency matters beyond highlight plays.

Example

Player with a 65% Success Rate vs. Player with a 45% Success Rate

The higher Success Rate player has produced efficient outcomes on a greater percentage of qualifying plays, providing useful context when evaluating baseline consistency.
