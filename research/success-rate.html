What is Success Rate? Consistency Explained

Success Rate measures how often a player or offense produces positive, efficient plays. It is a key indicator of stability in NFL performance.

Success Rate Definition

Understanding consistency over volatility.

Definition

Success Rate = percentage of plays that achieve positive expected value.

A play is considered “successful” if it gains meaningful yards relative to down and distance.

Why Success Rate Matters for Props

Consistency is often more important than explosive games.

01

Stability Indicator

High success rate players are more predictable week to week.

02

Reduces Volatility Risk

Helps identify players less dependent on big plays.

03

Drive Sustainability

Offenses with high success rate sustain longer drives.

04

Prop Safety Signal

Strong success rate = safer baseline for overs/unders.

Real NFL Example

Why success rate matters more than highlight plays.

Example

Player with 65% Success Rate vs 45% Success Rate

The higher success rate player consistently produces efficient plays, even if they don’t always have explosive performances.
