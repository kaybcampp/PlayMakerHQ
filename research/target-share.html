What is Target Share? WR Opportunity Explained

Target Share measures how often a player is looked at by the quarterback when they are running routes. It is one of the strongest indicators of wide receiver fantasy and prop production.

Target Share Definition

The clearest signal of WR opportunity in the passing game.

Definition

Target Share = percentage of team pass attempts directed at a player.

If a team throws the ball 40 times and a receiver gets 10 targets, their target share is 25%.

Why Target Share Matters for Props

Volume is more important than efficiency for predicting WR outcomes.

01

Volume Drives Consistency

High target share WRs get stable weekly opportunity.

02

Reduces Volatility

Even inefficient games still produce catches when targets are high.

03

Role Stability Signal

Coaches rarely remove high-target players from game plans.

04

Better Than Yards Alone

Targets predict future production better than box score stats.

Real NFL Example

How target share separates elite WRs from inconsistent ones.

Example

WR with 28% Target Share vs 14% Target Share

The 28% receiver is a primary offensive option and will see consistent volume regardless of game script. The 14% receiver is dependent on big plays and matchup variance.
