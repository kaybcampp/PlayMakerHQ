What is YAC? Yards After Catch Explained

Yards After Catch (YAC) measures how many yards a player gains after receiving the ball. It is a key indicator of playmaking ability and explosive production.

YAC Definition

Understanding post-catch production in NFL analytics.

Definition

YAC = yards gained after a receiver catches the ball.

It measures what happens AFTER the catch — including broken tackles, speed, and open-field ability.

Why YAC Matters for Props

Explosive players can outperform expectations with limited targets.

01

Explosive Play Creation

Players with high YAC turn short passes into big gains.

02

Efficiency Booster

YAC increases yardage even without deep targets.

03

Mismatch Indicator

Strong YAC players exploit weak tackling defenses.

04

Prop Volatility Signal

High YAC players can spike production unexpectedly.
