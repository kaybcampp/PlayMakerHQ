What is YPRR? Yards Per Route Run Explained

Yards Per Route Run (YPRR) measures how many yards a receiver generates for every route they run — one of the strongest indicators of WR efficiency in football analytics.

YPRR Definition

The most efficient WR production metric in NFL analytics.

Definition

YPRR = total receiving yards ÷ routes run.

It measures how productive a player is every time they run a route — not just when they get targeted.

Why YPRR Matters for Props

Efficiency often predicts breakout performance better than volume alone.

01

02

03

04

Real NFL Example

Why YPRR identifies hidden elite receivers.

Example

WR with 2.8 YPRR vs WR with 1.4 YPRR

The higher YPRR player produces nearly double the efficiency per route, meaning they generate more yards even without elite target volume.
