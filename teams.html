Teams Hub FAQ

How PlayMaker builds team intelligence, trends, and matchup context.

How are team trends calculated?

Team trends are generated from PlayMaker results, win/loss patterns, confidence scores, offensive and defensive matchups, and recent performance context across the season.

What are defensive tiers based on?

Defensive tiers are built using EPA allowed, pass defense efficiency, run defense efficiency, pressure rate, and performance against position-specific player types.

How does PlayMaker evaluate team matchups?

Matchups are evaluated by comparing offensive tendencies against defensive weaknesses, along with injury impact, pace, weather conditions, and historical performance trends.

How often are team reports updated?

Team reports update continuously during the season as new games, injuries, and PlayMaker results are processed into the system.

What data powers PlayMaker teams?

PlayMaker uses NFL play-by-play data, roster data, defensive tiers, player prop results, and matchup-level historical outcomes.

Why do team trends matter for player props?

Team trends help identify game environments that increase or decrease player opportunities, such as high-pass scripts, weak run defenses, or fast-paced offensive systems.
